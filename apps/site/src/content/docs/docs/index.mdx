Connect [App Store](/docs/connect-app-store/) or [Google Play](/docs/connect-google-play/), then [prepare and publish replies](/docs/manage-both-stores/) from one Reply Inbox. Use [Review analysis](/docs/review-analysis/) to explore recurring topics and reported severity.

Read [usage and limits](/docs/usage-limits/) for Cloud plans, or [self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting/) to operate your own installation. The [source repository](https://github.com/bibliocards/reviewinbox) contains development setup, architecture, and decision records.
