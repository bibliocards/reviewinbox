# ReviewInbox glossary

ReviewInbox turns public store Reviews into human-controlled replies and product insights.

## Language

**Organization**: The tenant that owns members, Apps, Store Connections, Reviews, and usage. Avoid: Workspace, account, team.

**Owner**: An Organization member with the owner role. Admins and ordinary members have separate permissions.

**App**: A mobile product with at most one Apple App Store and one Google Play Store Connection. Avoid: Project.

**Store Connection**: The configured link between an App and one store. Avoid: Integration, connector.

**Store Credential**: The secret material used by a Store Connection to read Reviews and publish replies. Avoid: Token, key when referring to the whole credential.

**Review**: A store review imported for an App and Store Connection. Avoid: Feedback, message, comment.

**Reply Inbox**: The workflow for drafting, editing, ignoring, and publishing replies to Reviews. Avoid: Feedback board, support inbox.

**Reply Draft**: Proposed reply text, generated by AI or written by a member, awaiting explicit publication. Avoid: Suggested answer, response draft.

**Published Reply**: The latest reply successfully published to the store. Avoid: Response, answer.

**Reply Context**: App-level guidance for drafting, such as known fixes and support policies. Avoid: Knowledge base, system prompt.

**Reply Language Policy**: An App's default reply language and additional supported languages.

**Review Intent**: One of the shared reasons expressed in a Review, such as requesting help or reporting a problem. A Review can have several intents.

**Review Topic**: A subject in one App's catalogue, shared across its Store Connections. A Review can discuss several topics.

**Topic Validation Status**: Whether a topic is pending, approved, or rejected by a human. This is separate from its assignment to a Review.

**Reported Severity**: The impact described in a Review, from no problem to critical, or unknown. It is not a star rating, verified incident, or reply priority.

**Sync Run**: One recorded attempt to retrieve Reviews from a Store Connection.

**Managed AI**: Draft generation funded by the Cloud operator and subject to the Organization's monthly quota.

**Usage Event**: A recorded action used for usage accounting, such as a Review import or successful AI draft generation.
