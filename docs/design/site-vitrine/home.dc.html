Questions

Does AI publish replies automatically?

No. AI drafting creates Reply Drafts only. Publishing remains an explicit action controlled by your team.

Can ReviewInbox be self-hosted?

Yes. The self-hosted stack runs with Docker Compose and keeps your data on your own infrastructure.

Which stores are supported?

App Store and Google Play. Each store has its own access and review-history limits.

How does Review analysis work?

Jev classifies imported Reviews by topic, intent and reported severity. Luna proposes missing topics for human approval. Analysis stays separate from drafting and never publishes replies.
