Billing questions

Who is a plan attached to?

Plans attach to Organizations, not individual users. A user can belong to several Organizations, each with its own limits and usage.

Do usage limits apply when self-hosting?

No. Usage limits apply to Cloud deployments only. Self-hosted deployments stay billing-free.
